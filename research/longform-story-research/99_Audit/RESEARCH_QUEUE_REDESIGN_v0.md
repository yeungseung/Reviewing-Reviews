> Status: PROVISIONAL  
> This document does not replace the original 86-item Master List. It records how the remaining #60–#86 research should be reconsidered in light of the audit.

## 1. Why the queue should change

The original plan contains 92 uncompleted Themes.

The audit now shows:
- several v2 categories are downstream expressions of the same recurring mechanisms
- the same original paper was often re-registered under multiple SRC IDs
- exact quantitative claims are much less reliable than qualitative mechanisms
- worksheets/maps/checklists are product instruments rather than ordinary academic hypotheses
- synthesis/meta-model items should not be researched before the mechanism map is cleaned

Therefore the remaining queue should not simply continue #60 → #86 unchanged.

# 2. Proposed execution order

## WAVE 1 — Epistemic Integrity / Trust
Priority: HIGHEST

### #60 Evidence Layer
Disposition: **KEEP + STRENGTHEN**

Research questions should emphasize:
- evidence taxonomy
- primary vs secondary evidence
- descriptive fact vs inference vs forecast
- certainty calibration
- anecdote/generalization limits
- disagreement and uncertainty representation

Required source policy:
- primary methodological/epistemic sources first
- no Story Engine application until evidence categories are settled

### #61 Emotional framing and factual distortion
Disposition: **KEEP + REFRAME**

Separate two questions:
1. empirical effects of emotional framing on judgment/comprehension
2. normative/professional standards for truthful storytelling

Do not mix "ethical recommendation" with "audience effect size."

### #62 Counterargument
Disposition: **MERGE CANDIDATE with #63**

Focus on:
- when counterarguments improve calibration/trust
- when they create confusion
- motivated reasoning
- inoculation / two-sided messages
- false balance

### #63 Counterpoint
Disposition: **MERGE CANDIDATE with #62**

The current #62/#63 distinction may be too editorial.
A more useful parent question may be:

> How should a Story Engine represent competing explanations, counterevidence, uncertainty, and alternative causal models without destroying narrative coherence?

Suggested output:
**Epistemic Counterpoint Module**

### Recommended Wave 1 package

Instead of 12 independent Themes, consider a smaller high-rigor package:

1. Evidence type & hierarchy
2. Certainty / attribution language
3. Emotional framing & judgment distortion
4. Alternative explanations & causal inference
5. Counterargument / two-sided message effects
6. False balance / misleading symmetry
7. Source transparency & correction
8. Narrative coherence under uncertainty

Final count should be fixed only after the current audit completes.

**Literature hypothesis → Engine feature → measurable content annotation → audience outcome**
