# RELEVANCE & HUMAN-SCALE TAXONOMY v0

> Purpose: distinguish mechanisms currently collapsed into "Viewer Relevance" or "Human Scale."

| Code | Mechanism | Primary question | Useful engine role | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Identifiability | Does a concrete individual change affect/valuation? | human consequence lens | bias, scope neglect |
| R2 | Psychological distance | How near/far and concrete/abstract is the representation? | zoom level / construal control | assuming concrete is always superior |
| R3 | Scope sensitivity / psychic numbing | Does affect scale with number/magnitude? | macro↔micro framing | manipulating feeling at expense of proportionality |
| R4 | Risk perception | Which qualitative properties make a hazard feel risky? | stakes/threat framing | confusing perceived risk with objective risk |
| R5 | Self relevance | Why does this matter to this viewer? | relevance/mirror | forced personalization |
| R6 | Communicative relevance | Is the cognitive payoff worth the processing effort? | information selection | oversimplification |

## Proposed engine behavior

A future engine should not always "humanize."

It should choose a lens:

### System lens
Use when causal structure, scale, or policy/system mechanism matters.

### Individual lens
Use when concrete lived consequence is necessary to understand impact.

### Comparative lens
Move between one case and aggregate evidence to avoid anecdotal distortion.

### Viewer lens
Connect implications to the audience only when a genuine relationship exists.

### Risk lens
Separate:
- objective probability/consequence
- perceived dread/unknownness
- narrative stakes

## Recommended evidence rule for nonfiction

A vivid case should never silently substitute for population-level evidence.

Preferred sequence when both matter:

```
AGGREGATE FACT
   ↓
IDENTIFIABLE EXAMPLE
   ↓
RETURN TO AGGREGATE
   ↓
LIMIT / REPRESENTATIVENESS CHECK
```

This gives emotional concreteness without allowing one anecdote to become the dataset.
