## Interpretation

A source appearing across many Tracks should trigger two separate actions:

1. **Concept audit** — determine whether several legacy categories are actually expressions of one parent mechanism.
2. **Independence audit** — search for additional independent studies/research traditions before raising confidence.

This file is therefore a bridge from source reuse to Research Map consolidation, not an evidence-strength leaderboard.
