## Interpretation rule

A source family repeatedly appearing across Themes should trigger two separate questions:

1. **Mechanism recurrence:** Why does this paper/theory keep becoming relevant across different story questions?
2. **Evidence independence:** Are there genuinely independent studies or research traditions supporting the same mechanism?

Only the second question can raise evidence confidence.
